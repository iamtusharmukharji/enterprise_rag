# Procurement Policy

## Purpose
Procurement controls vendor selection, purchase authorization, and financial commitments.

## Purchase Orders
A purchase order is required before committing the company to purchases above ₹25,000 unless an approved exception applies.

Employees must not split one purchase into multiple transactions to avoid an approval threshold.

## Approval Thresholds
Purchases up to ₹25,000 require manager approval.

Purchases above ₹25,000 and up to ₹100,000 require manager and department-head approval.

Purchases above ₹100,000 require Finance approval in addition to the business approvals.

## Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must complete vendor onboarding before a purchase order is issued. Finance verifies required tax and banking information.

Security review is required when a vendor will access confidential data, production systems, or privileged company resources.

## Competitive Quotes
For purchases above ₹100,000, Procurement should obtain at least 3 competitive quotations when the market permits.

A sole-source purchase requires a documented business justification and Procurement approval.

## Emergency Purchases
Emergency purchases may bypass normal pre-approval when delaying the purchase would create a material operational or security risk. The employee must document the emergency and obtain retrospective approval within 2 business days.

## Related Policies
Expense Policy governs reimbursement and ordinary expense approvals. Security Guidelines apply when a supplier receives system or data access.
