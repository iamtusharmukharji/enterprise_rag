# General Expense Policy

## Purpose
The Expense Policy governs ordinary business expenses that are not covered by a dedicated benefit or travel rule.

## Approval Thresholds
Expenses up to ₹10,000 require manager approval.

Expenses above ₹10,000 and up to ₹50,000 require manager and department-head approval.

Expenses above ₹50,000 require manager, department-head, and Finance approval.

## Receipts
Receipts are mandatory for expenses of ₹500 or more unless Finance has explicitly approved an exception.

A business purpose must be recorded for every reimbursed expense.

## Submission
Expenses should be submitted within 30 calendar days. Claims older than 60 calendar days require Finance approval and may be rejected.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal entertainment, traffic penalties, alcohol, personal subscriptions, and expenses incurred for another person's private use are not reimbursable.

## Corporate Cards
Corporate-card transactions must be reconciled within 10 business days after the monthly statement is issued.

Employees remain responsible for documenting the business purpose even when a corporate card is used.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from Finance and should identify the policy rule being overridden and the business reason.

## Related Policies
Travel-specific limits override this policy for approved business travel. The Reimbursement Policy defines special employee benefits such as home-office equipment and internet reimbursement.
