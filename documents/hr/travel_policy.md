# Business Travel Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines approval, transportation, lodging, meals, and documentation requirements for business travel.

## Travel Approval
Domestic travel should be approved by the employee's manager before booking. International travel requires manager approval and department-head approval.

Travel for customer engagements should include the customer or project reference in the travel request.

## Air Travel
Economy class is the default for flights. Employees may book premium economy for flights longer than 6 hours with manager approval.

Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 10 hours when the department head approves the exception.

## Hotels
Employees should use company-preferred hotels where available. The standard lodging ceiling is ₹8,000 per night for domestic travel, excluding applicable taxes.

A higher hotel rate requires documented justification and manager approval.

## Meals
Employees may claim up to ₹1,500 per day for meals during domestic business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

When a customer or company event provides a meal, that meal cannot also be claimed as an individual travel expense.

## Local Transport
Reasonable taxi, public transportation, and airport-transfer expenses are reimbursable when supported by receipts or approved travel records.

## International Travel
International travel uses the same approval hierarchy but may apply country-specific lodging and meal limits maintained by Finance.

## Submission
Travel expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 calendar days after returning from travel.

## Related Policies
Procurement rules apply to travel vendors when a purchase requires formal sourcing. Expense Policy requirements apply where this policy does not specify a travel-specific limit.
