# Employee Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines eligible employee reimbursements and approval requirements.

## Home-Office Equipment
Employees approved for remote or hybrid work may claim up to ₹25,000 for approved ergonomic home-office equipment once every 24 months.

Eligible items include an ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, desk lamp, and approved ergonomic accessories.

The ₹25,000 limit is cumulative across eligible equipment purchases during the 24-month period. It is not a per-item limit.

A purchase above the remaining allowance requires the employee to pay the difference unless Finance grants a documented exception.

## Internet
Employees in approved remote arrangements may claim up to ₹1,500 per month for home internet when the expense is included in the employee's approved remote-work benefit. Mobile data top-ups are not reimbursable under this benefit.

## Business Meals
Business meals with external customers are reimbursable up to ₹2,000 per attendee per meal in India, excluding alcohol. Expenses above the limit require department-head approval before reimbursement.

## Submission Deadline
Reimbursement claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 60 days require Finance approval and may be rejected.

Receipts are mandatory for individual expenses of ₹500 or more. For expenses below ₹500, a receipt is preferred but not mandatory if the expense is otherwise verifiable.

## Approval
The employee's manager approves ordinary business expenses. Finance performs policy validation and payment processing. Procurement approval may also be required for purchases that meet procurement thresholds.

## Related Policies
Procurement rules govern vendor onboarding and purchase approvals. Expense Policy rules apply to business expenses not covered by a specific reimbursement benefit.
