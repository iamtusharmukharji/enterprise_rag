# Employee Leave Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines paid and unpaid leave available to full-time employees of Northstar Systems. It applies to employees in India unless a local employment agreement explicitly provides a more favorable statutory benefit.

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 24 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 working days per completed month. Employees joining after 1 July receive a prorated annual entitlement.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 12 working days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 12 days expires on 31 March unless HR approves an exception.

Annual leave requests of 1–2 working days should normally be submitted at least 2 business days before the first day of leave. Requests of 3 or more working days should normally be submitted at least 7 business days in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate. Managers may request HR review when repeated short-duration sick leave creates an operational concern.

Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is not paid out when employment ends.

## Parental and Paternity Leave
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of maternity leave in accordance with applicable Indian law and company benefits.

Eligible employees receive 15 working days of paid paternity leave. Paternity leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or legal adoption.

Parental leave should be coordinated with the employee's manager and HR so that project coverage can be arranged.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees may take 5 working days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member. HR may approve up to 3 additional working days for exceptional travel requirements.

## Leave Without Pay
Leave without pay may be approved when paid leave balances are exhausted. Approval requires the manager and HR. Leave without pay longer than 10 consecutive working days also requires department-head approval.

## Approval and Records
The employee's manager approves normal leave requests. HR handles statutory leave, extended leave, and exceptions. The HR system is the system of record for balances and approvals.

## Related Policies
Remote employees should consult the Remote Work Policy for work-location rules. Reimbursement of approved home-office equipment is governed by the Reimbursement Policy, not this document.
