# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose
The Remote and Hybrid Work Policy defines eligibility, scheduling, security, and equipment expectations for employees working away from a company office.

## Eligibility
Employees may request a hybrid arrangement after completing 90 days of employment. Eligibility depends on role requirements, customer commitments, security requirements, and manager approval.

Roles requiring dedicated laboratory, hardware, or on-site customer access may be designated office-based.

## Hybrid Schedule
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams may establish fixed collaboration days when project coordination requires in-person attendance.

A manager may temporarily require additional office attendance for onboarding, incidents, customer workshops, or critical project activities.

## Home Office
Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace and reliable internet connection. The company may provide standard equipment such as a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset.

Approved ergonomic home-office equipment is reimbursed under the Reimbursement Policy. The Remote Work Policy does not define reimbursement limits.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-managed devices and the approved VPN when accessing internal systems from an untrusted network. Devices must use disk encryption, screen locking, and endpoint security controls.

Employees must not connect company devices to public computers or install unapproved software. Sensitive information must not be stored in personal cloud drives.

## Internet and Power
Employees are expected to maintain a reasonable working internet connection and backup power arrangement where practical. Internet reimbursement is available only when explicitly approved under the applicable reimbursement program.

## Exceptions
Temporary remote-work exceptions may be approved by the manager for up to 30 calendar days. Longer exceptions require HR approval.

## Related Policies
Security requirements are detailed in the Security Guidelines. Home-office reimbursement limits are defined in the Reimbursement Policy. Leave rules remain governed by the Leave Policy.
